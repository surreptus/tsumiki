## Privacy

**This site does not collect your data.** It has no accounts, forms, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it sets no cookies.

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| What do I type into the site? | The search box and the conjugator. Both run in your browser. What you type is never sent to a server. |
| What does the site store on my device? | One setting, your light or dark theme choice, in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and you can clear it at any time. |
| Does it load anything from other sites? | No. The fonts, search index and scripts are all served from this site. |
| What does the "Copy the prompt" button do? | It copies text to your clipboard. Nothing is sent from the site. If you paste the prompt into an AI service, that service's own privacy policy applies to what you type there. |
| Who sees that I visited? | The host. The site is served by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address when you load a page. That is covered by [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), not by this site. |
| Do you share data with anyone? | There is no data to share. |

If you follow a link to another site, that site's own privacy policy applies.

If this changes, for example if analytics are added, this page will be updated first and will say what is collected and why.
