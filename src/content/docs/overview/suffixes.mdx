A **suffix** is the other half of the idea: an ending that takes one specific stem and returns a new word. Every suffix can be described by three facts.

<SuffixesArt />

| Fact | For たい ("want to") |
| --- | --- |
| Attaches to which stem | 連用形, the ます-stem |
| Takes which type | a verb |
| Gives which type | an い-adjective |

## One suffix, one stem

This is the rule that does most of the work. たい takes the ます-stem, ない takes the ない-stem, ば takes the ば-stem. They never swap. That is why 書きない and 書かたい are wrong without needing a separate rule for each: the suffix simply does not accept that stem.

## A suffix can change shape

Some suffixes have two shapes, chosen by the sound of the stem in front of them.

| Suffix | After an ichidan stem | After a godan stem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| passive | 食べ**られ**る | 書か**れ**る |
| causative | 食べ**させ**る | 書か**せ**る |
| volitional | 食べ**よう** | 書こ**う** |

It is still one suffix. The shape is just chosen by what comes before it.

## Some forms are the stem alone

The て-form and the past (た-form) need no ending at all, because the stem already includes it. They are still functions, and you can chain after them: `past(neg(食べる))` is 食べなかった.

## The same job in different words

The idea of "negative" is one function, but it reaches the same result by different routes depending on what you give it:

| Put in | Route | Get out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| a verb (食べる) | ない-stem + ない | 食べない |
| an い-adjective (高い) | stem + くない | 高くない |
| a noun or な-adjective (静か) | word + じゃない | 静かじゃない |

<Derivation verb="静か" chain={["neg", "past"]} />

Here the noun-like 静か turns into an い-adjective by the first step, so the past follows the い-adjective route: なかった. The type changed, and that decided the next step.
