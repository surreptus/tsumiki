Each page also shows when it was last updated. This list covers the changes a returning reader would want to know about.

## 31 October 2026

- Every example now shows its reading in hiragana.
- The conjugator refuses forms that pass the type check but are not used in Japanese, and says why: ある has no potential, 寝る takes お休みになる, an ending does not stack on itself, and passive and potential do not stack. いい + そう is now よさそう, and ので can follow a polite form (食べますので).
- Error messages name the kind of problem (wrong kind of word, wrong order, not used) instead of always saying "type error".
- Conjugator buttons and sidebar labels show a short English meaning, such as "たい · want to", and the palette groups say where each ending attaches. The adjective-stem endings are in the palette too.
- Ending pages show a godan verb in the Construction table, so the stem change (書く → 書き) is visible.
- Clearer wording on the idea, stems, suffixes and types pages about what a stem is, the て-form, and the ます exception.
- Wide tables scroll sideways on a phone instead of breaking Japanese words.
- The AI tutor prompt says the notes are unreviewed, gives readings by default, and has a level menu.
- Pages load less: the fonts' stylesheet now includes only the characters the site uses.
- Search results and link previews show the English meaning of each ending.

## 5 November 2026

- Added the short causative-passive される for godan verbs (書かされる, 待たされる). Verified with the engine; the conjugator now offers it and refuses it for す-verbs, ichidan and irregular verbs, saying what to use instead.
- Honorific verbs (いらっしゃる, なさる, おっしゃる, 下さる) are no longer made causative.

## 29 October 2026

- Every ending, stem and guide page now has a "Practise this with an AI tutor" box with a copy-paste prompt. The prompt gives the tutor the page's own rules and examples and asks it to teach by questions, in a hard and adaptive way.
- The sidebar is regrouped: Overview, Tools and quick help, Reference.
- The home page shows the function call with a label on each part.
- Fixed illustrations that had lost their arrows and marks.

## 28 October 2026

- Added [Find by meaning](/reference/by-meaning/), every ending grouped by what it says.
- Added [Common mistakes](/overview/common-mistakes/) and a printable [cheat sheet](/overview/cheat-sheet/).
- The home page now says the Japanese has not yet been reviewed by a native speaker.
- Added a [Privacy and terms of use](/privacy-and-terms/) page.
- Added [Using this with a course](/overview/with-a-course/), on how to use the site beside a textbook.
- Added a "Report a mistake" link at the bottom of every page, and a link to the project on GitHub.
- The conjugator now dims suffixes that would be a type error as the next step, and Undo and Clear are the same size.
- The types illustration now shows each result's type under its block and uses たい, ない and た for the labels.
- Pages print in light colours with the controls left out.

## 27 October 2026

- First public version: the reference for every stem and suffix, the conjugator, the overview pages and seven guides (particles, sentence-ending particles, keigo, casual speech, clauses, conditionals, special expressions).
- ○ and × replace the check and cross marks.
- Repeated explanations were removed across pages, and the home page was rewritten around what the guide gives a learner.

Japanese on this site has not yet been reviewed by a native speaker. When corrections are made, they will be listed here.
