## Original writing

The explanations, usage notes, comparisons and example sentences were written for this project.
They are not copied or closely paraphrased from any textbook or website. Japanese grammar itself, such
as how a verb conjugates, is a set of facts about a language and belongs to nobody. The way this guide
organises those facts, as typed function composition, is its own.

## Influences

These works shaped what this guide covers. None of their text is reproduced here.

- **Tae Kim, *A Guide to Japanese Grammar*.** The scope of the guide follows Tae Kim's, and it
  is the reason so many learners can think about Japanese grammar in terms of building blocks. His guide
  is published under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 at [guidetojapanese.org](https://guidetojapanese.org).
- ***Genki: An Integrated Course in Elementary Japanese* I and II** (The Japan Times). Used to check for gaps in coverage.
- The traditional Japanese school-grammar stem names (連用形, 未然形, 仮定形, 命令形 and so on), which are public
  terminology.
